Our Mission

Built for students who deserve more.

AllUs was founded in 2022 by Jacob Rivera, then a freshman at a STEM-specialized high school in Puerto Rico, to make college and career guidance accessible to students who had nowhere else to turn.

What started as a local initiative grew into a structured program now serving two schools in East Puerto Rico — and counting. We believe that a student's zip code should never determine their ceiling.

Every service we offer — tutoring, scholarship advising, academic planning — is and will always be completely free.

🎓

College Advising

From College Board prep to application strategy, we guide students through every step of the admissions process.

🔬

STEM Tutoring

1-on-1 peer tutoring in math, science, and engineering subjects — from a team that knows these subjects from the inside out.

💰

Scholarship Navigation

We help students find, apply for, and secure aid — demystifying a process that stops too many students from even trying.

🗺️

Career Path Mapping

Personalized academic roadmaps built for each student's interests, skills, and goals within Puerto Rico's UPR system and beyond.
